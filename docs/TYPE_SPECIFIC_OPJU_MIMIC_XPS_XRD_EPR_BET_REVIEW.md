# Type-Specific OPJU Mimic Review

## Current-Round Types

- BET.
- XPS in-situ.
- XPS non-in-situ.
- XRD.
- EPR.

The current-round specifications live under `src/origin/type_mimic/` and are
registered through `TYPE_MIMIC_SPECS`.

## Excluded Types

HPLC/LC, Raman, EIS, Mott-Schottky, UV-vis DRS/Tauc, PL, photocurrent, FTIR,
and reaction-condition screening are not in this product pilot.

## Per-Type Safety Notes

- BET: no pore-structure or BET model-validity conclusion.
- XPS in-situ: no valence, electron-transfer, or S-scheme conclusion.
- XPS non-in-situ: no valence, electron-transfer, or S-scheme conclusion.
- XRD: no phase/crystal-plane assignment and no automatic reference-card copying.
- EPR: no radical assignment or mechanism conclusion.

All generated graphs are visual-calibration products requiring manual review.
